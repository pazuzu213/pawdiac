Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Pawdiac helps dog owners understand their dog's moods, energy, and daily patterns through a personalized cosmic profile and owner-provided observations.

Information We Collect

We may collect your email address, your dog's name, birthday, photo, breed, personality traits, reading history, notification preferences, and observations you choose to log about your dog's behavior.

How We Use Information

We use this information to create and improve personalized Pawdiac readings, maintain your dog profile, operate waitlist and account features, send requested notifications, improve the product, and provide support.

AI and Personalization

Pawdiac may use AI systems to generate personalized readings from your dog's profile, daily cosmic conditions, and optional observation data. We do not use your data to make medical, veterinary, or emergency decisions.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with service providers that help us operate Pawdiac, such as hosting, analytics, email delivery, push notifications, and AI infrastructure.

Your Choices

You may request deletion of your waitlist or account data by contacting support. You can also control notification permissions through your device settings.

Children

Pawdiac is not intended for children under 13 and is not made for kids.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact: support@pawdiac.app
